Privacy policy

What the portfolio collects

You can browse the portfolio without an account. It has no sign-in, checkout, contact form, advertising or analytics run by me, and it doesn’t set cookies or save anything in your browser’s storage. The project filters, the featured carousel and the demos all run in your browser and don’t send what you do anywhere.

The copy button in the contact section only puts my email address on your clipboard when you press it.

Hosting and fonts

GitHub Pages hosts the portfolio, including its images and screen recordings. GitHub says it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security, even when they’re not signed in to GitHub, and handles those logs under its own privacy practices.
